# 🏎️ Grand Prix Racing Simulation Pro

A feature-rich, high-octane 2D top-down car racing simulation game built entirely using **Python's Turtle Graphics** framework. Place your bets, adapt to unpredictable weather conditions, and watch custom retro-sprites battle it out on a dynamic leaderboard!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **🎮 Interactive Economy System:** Start with 100 coins. Place your stakes wisely on your favorite color before each race. Don't go bankrupt!
* **🌦️ Dynamic Weather Engine:** Experience dynamic track variations—**Sunny**, **Rainy**, or **Night Mode**. Rain conditions realistically slow down maximum traction, adding a tactical layer to predictions.
* **📊 Live Ranking Tracker:** A real-time telemetry sidebar tracks car positions instantly on screen during the race.
* **🏁 Cinematic Startup:** Includes a fully synchronized visual and acoustic **3-2-1-GO!** countdown ticker.
* **⚡ Core Power-ups:** Keep an eye out for randomly generated **🪙 Coins** on tracks that provide a massive speed boost, alongside random **Nitro Boosts**.
* **🏆 Victory Podium:** At the end of each race, the top 3 cars are teleported onto a custom-rendered 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place podium.
* **💾 Persistent Leaderboard:** Saves all-time color statistics inside a flat-file database (`race_leaderboard.txt`), keeping score even after closing the program.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Python 3** (Core Language)
* **Turtle Graphics** (Standard GUI framework)
* **Winsound Module** (Hardware-synchronized audio effects)
* **OS & Time Libraries** (Data stream handling and loop delays)

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Clone the repository and move your 5 custom `.gif` car sprites (`car_red.gif`, `car_green.gif`, etc.) into the main project directory.
2. Run `main.py` using your local Python interpreter.
3. Enter your choice of car color in the pop-up field.
4. Input your bidding volume based on your current wallet.
5. Watch the race unfold and secure your podium finish!
